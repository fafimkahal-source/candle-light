OUR STORY

From One Beginning To Three Branches

Candle Light began with a simple idea: to create beautiful lighting pieces that bring elegance, warmth and personality into every home.

Through dedication, quality craftsmanship and the trust of our customers, that small beginning continued to grow. Today, Candle Light proudly serves its customers through three branches.

We do more than simply offer chandeliers. Candle Light manufactures its own pieces and creates custom designs according to each customer's vision, space and style.
